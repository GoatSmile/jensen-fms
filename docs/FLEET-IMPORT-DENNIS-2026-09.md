## B · Five rules I will follow — unless you tell me otherwise

1. **Department unclear →** the bike goes on the customer (e.g. *Gladsaxe
   Kommune*), and we put it on the right department later — on site, or with
   you. Your sheet's department names often differ from the ones in e-conomic.
2. **A frame listed twice** (32 frames — list at the back) → I keep the row with
   the latest delivery date; the other row stays in the bike's history. Most look
   like a bike that moved from one customer to another. Correct any you know.
3. **The same battery or charger number on two bikes** (17 numbers — list at the
   back) → the number goes on the newest bike; the other gets a note. The system
   allows each number only once.
4. **Your words become the bike's status:** *stjålet* or *kan ikke findes* →
   lost or stolen (17 bikes); *udgået* → retired (8 bikes); everything else → in
   service with the customer.
5. **Old models** (V1–V8, Svajer V4 and V6) keep their model name as text. They
   do not need a template — templates are for bikes we build now.
